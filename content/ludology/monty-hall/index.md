# The Monty Hall Problem

This article is about walking people through an unintuitive math trick.  Monty Hall was a host on a game show called Let's Make A Deal and actually not a mathmetician.  

## The Situation

I present to you three doors, of which only one contains a prize.  You choose a door, and I reveal one of the other two that I know is empty.  When I offer you to switch doors, you say _YES_!!!  

Here's why:

## The Math

The door you picked has a 1/3 chance of winning.  That also means there's a **2/3 chance it is NOT your door**.  When I reveal the empty door, there is still a 2/3 chance it is NOT your door despite the new information.  

This means you have 1/3 chance to win a prize if you stay, and a 2/3 chance to win if you switch doors.
