# 10 Great Games That Use Cards

[< Back Home](/)

I'm naturally drawn to cardplay mechanics.  They have a sense of RNG that seems fair, especially when you know what's in the deck.  There's a need for resource management and optimizing your turn based on what's available in your hand.  There's a great deal of replayability because every session plays out a little bit differently based on luck of the draw.  

I present to you 10 games that are exceptionally good card games or use cards in interesting ways.

## Slay the Spire

![Slay the Spire](/images/game-capsules/slay-the-spire.jpg)

Arguably the best deckbuilding game of all time, excited for the sequel.

## Inscryption

![Inscryption](/images/game-capsules/inscryption.jpg)

One of my favourites, it's the game within a game that becomes 3 different games and then gains sentience and self-deletes.  

## Balatro

![Balatro](/images/game-capsules/balatro.jpg)

Solid deckbuilder game design, poker is easily recognizable and number go **biiiiig**.

## Baten Kaitos

![Baten Kaitos](/images/game-capsules/baten-kaitos.jpg)

The cards combo together and age, quick-paced combat.  Comes with a good JRPG story.

## Monster Train

![Monster Train](/images/game-capsules/monster-train.jpg)

Takes the smash deck approach to deckbuilding and includes a light tower defense element.

## Card Shark

![Card Shark](/images/game-capsules/card-shark.jpg)

You have to manipulate positions of cards using quick memorization and slight of hand to cheat and progress in the story.

## Mega Man Battle Network

![Mega Man Battle Network](/images/game-capsules/megaman-battle-network.jpg)

Cards (battle chips) are your loadout for a 'round' of real time combat, combotastic.

## Power Chord

![Power Chord](/images/game-capsules/power-chord.jpg)

Party based deckbuilder, and I'm a sucker for heavy metal.

## Death Roads: Tournament

![Death Roads Tournament](/images/game-capsules/death-roads.jpg)

Deck is populated by cards attached to the parts you pick out for your car.  Kind of another deckbuilding roguelite but I like execution of the theme.
