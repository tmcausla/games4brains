# 10 Great Sports Games That Aren't Real Sports

Really more of a clever title than a genre I passionately seek out.  Oh well let's go through em.

I present to you 10 games about sports that aren't actually sports (sometimes they kinda are).

## Blood Bowl 2

![Blood Bowl 2](/images/game-covers/blood-bowl2.jpg)

Imagine if American football could be more violent, and then add spikes to all the players' padding.  Plays way faster than on tabletop.

## Rocket League

![Rocket League](/images/game-covers/rocket-league.jpg)

This was a rare moment when I got really into an online game.  It's amazing to go from weak hops to aerial acrobatics to make big plays.

## NFL Street 2

![NFL Street 2](/images/game-covers/nfl-street2.jpg)

Yeah sure the NFL exists, **but not like this**.  You can run along a wall or slam someone into it without getting a flag on the play.

## Star Wars Episode I Racer

![Star Wars Episode I Racer](/images/game-covers/starwars-ep1-racer.jpg)

The future of racing?  Let's hope not.

## Fall Guys

![Fall Guys](/images/game-covers/fall-guys.jpg)

This is considered a sport the same way Most Extreme Elimination Challenge is considered a sport (it's not).

## Smash it Wild

![Smash it Wild](/images/game-covers/smash-it-wild.jpg)

Turn based tactics in volleyball?  It slows down the sport it's based off of but really makes it work (and more violent).

### A SUIVRE...

Maybe consider games like Quidditch World Cup, Super Dodgeball Advance.  Gotta find more games in the genre.
