This article is about walking people through an unintuitive math trick. Monty Hall was a host on a game show called Let's Make A Deal and actually not a mathmetician.

The Situation

I present to you three doors, of which only one contains a prize. You choose a door, and I reveal one of the other two that I know is empty. When I offer you to switch doors, you say YES!!!

Here's why:

The Math

The door you picked has a 1/3 chance of being the winning door. That also means there's a 2/3 chance it is NOT your door. When I reveal the empty door, there is still a 2/3 chance it is NOT your door despite the new information.

This means you have 1/3 chance to win a prize if you stay with your first choice, and a 2/3 chance to win if you switch doors.

The Psychology

How many people would choose to switch their doors? Unless you can math out precisely what's happening, it presents as a 50/50 chance whether you switch or not. There's a very human element that gets played on by the offer to switch: ego.

Many people are prone to keeping their first pick simply because it's theirs. They chose it. They've developed an emotional attachment to remain with it because it was their choice.
