10 Great Games That Will Trap You With "One More Turn"

I like a good turn based game that can rope you into the flow state and make you curse when you look at the clock at 3 in the morning.

I present to you 10 games that will trap you in their gameplay loops.

Sid Meier's Civilization VI

{{ img of CIV }}

The classic and most appropriate for this list. Specifically includes a clock in-game so that you can only blame yourself for losing track.

Slay the Spire

{{ img of Slay the Spire }}

It's too easy to start a new battle after you promised to stop after this one. Good pick for this list, kinda feel like I don't wanna put it on 2 lists though.

Balatro

{{ Balatro img }}

Same deal, the gameplay is quick but it's already on another list.

XCOM 2

{{ img of XCOM 2 }}

You can save and quit at any point during the game, but what if you took just one more action? Just hope it's not a miss.

Total War WarHammer II

{{ img of TWWH2 }}

"Just one more battle" can easily turn into 45 mins of micromanaging units on the battlefield.

Into the Breach

{{ img of Into the Breach }}

Turns are short and punchy so it's easy to pick up and play, but you have to put it down first.

Picross

{{ img of Picross }}

Definitely a personal pick. My muscle memory will start working a new puzzle before I have a chance to decide if I'm done.

Peglin

{{ img of Peglin }}

You'll find a new ball or fully upgrade an old one and you'll want to immediately find out what it does.

Diablo III

{{ img of Diablo III }}

You're more likely to start another mission when the last one gave you weak-ass loot.

SteamWorld Heist II

{{ img of SteamWorld Heist II}}

It's honestly just a well made turn based shooter, so yeah you end up doing another battle or two before you're actually ready to be done.
