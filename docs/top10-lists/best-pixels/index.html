10 Great Games With The Best Pixels

< Back Home

When I'm talking about the best pixels, I don't just mean they create a pretty picture. I'm talking about pixels that do heavy lifting: they create an emotional response bigger than their simple presentation would imply, or they're an integral part of the game being played.

I present to you 10 games with pixel graphics that wield their pixels mightily.

Noita

Every pixel is a material that interacts with the player and the environment. Hand it's stupid hard.

FAITH The Unholy Trilogy

The horror experience is truly greater than what its lofi design should be capable of but it's genuinely terrifying.

Animal Well

These pixels went big mode. It's a creepy and oddly serene beauty that fits in less than 35MB.

Dave the Diver(?)

I kinda just love the characters, game was a surprise hit with me and I wanna talk about it lol.

Hyper Light Drifter (?)

Sharp controls and it's a minimalist design for the most part but it goes hards for the cut scenes.

Sea of Stars (?)

Probably gets replaced, really just appreciate the art style and reminisce about the glory days of SNES RPG's (aka Chrono Trigger).

Gato Roboto (?)

It's just a cute metroidvania with literally only black and white pixels (or whatever filter you use).
